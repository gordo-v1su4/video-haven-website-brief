# DVD Collection

The Video Haven collection presents titles and episodes through DVD packaging and cover artwork.

## Language

**Release**:
A distinct title or episode in the Video Haven collection, including one whose video is not available yet. Alternate cover artwork for the same video belongs to the same release.
_Avoid_: Cover, edition (when referring to the video itself)

**Cover edition**:
A presentation of a release with its own cover artwork. Several cover editions can belong to one release without being different episodes.
_Avoid_: Episode, separate release (when only the artwork changes)
